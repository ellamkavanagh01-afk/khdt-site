Your questions answered

FAQs

How do I book?

The easiest way to book is through Viator, GetYourGuide, or TripAdvisor — all linked in the booking section below. You can also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp at +353 87 623 0932.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes — our tours focus on real history and folklore with a spooky atmosphere rather than jump scares. We can adjust the tone for younger groups with advance notice. Children 5–11 are €12, under 5s are free.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable shoes are a must — parts of the old town have cobblestones. Bring a warm layer and a small umbrella if rain is forecast. The route is mostly flat with regular stops so you can relax and take it all in.

Is it accessible?

Parts of the old town have cobbles and narrow paths. Please let us know any requirements in advance and we will plan the most suitable route for your group.

What happens in bad weather?

Tours run in most conditions — a bit of Kilkenny rain only adds to the atmosphere! In the case of severe weather we will contact you with options to reschedule or receive a full refund.

Can we book a private group tour?

Absolutely — private group tours are available by arrangement. Group discounts apply for 6 or more guests. Contact us directly via email or WhatsApp to discuss dates and rates.
